FitForm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

FitForm is a Chrome extension that shows you your estimated clothing size on shopping product pages. Your privacy is central to how it works: FitForm does not collect, transmit or sell any of your data.

What FitForm stores

When you save your measurements (gender selection, bust or chest, waist, hips and height), they are stored only in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage. They never leave your device, and FitForm has no servers, accounts or databases.

What FitForm reads

On https pages, FitForm checks for basic product-page signals (such as page metadata that marks a page as a product) to decide whether to show the size badge. This check happens entirely in your browser. FitForm does not record, store or transmit the pages you visit or their content.

What FitForm does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share user data.
  • It does not load or run remote code.

Deleting your data

You can remove your saved measurements at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing its data in Chrome.

Accuracy of sizes

Sizes shown are estimates based on your measurements and published size charts. Fit varies by brand and garment.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
